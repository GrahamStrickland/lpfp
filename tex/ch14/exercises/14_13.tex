% Exercise 14.13
In Figure~\ref{fig14_13}, we have a plot of the results of approximating the velocity
of a 1-kg object experiencing a net for 
\[
    F_{\text{net}}^{tv}(t,v_0) = -\alpha v_0,
\]
for $\alpha = 1 \,\text{N s/m}$, subject to the initial condition 
$v(0\, \text{s}) = 8\, \text{m}/\text{s}$
using the Euler method with $dt = 0.1$ and $dt = 0.5$ compared with the exact solution
\[
    v(t) = (8\, \text{m}/\text{s})e^{-\alpha t}.
\]

\begin{figure}[!ht]
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth, center]{../plots/14_13.pdf}
    \caption{Euler method compared with exact solution \label{fig14_13}} 
\end{figure}
