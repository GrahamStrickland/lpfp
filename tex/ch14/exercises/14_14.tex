In Figure~\ref{fig14_14}, we have a plot of the approximating to the velocity function
which is the solution of the differential equation 
\[
    \frac{dv(t)}{dt} = \cos{(t + v(t))},
\]
subject to the initial condition $v(0) = 0$, with $0 \leq t \leq 3$.

\begin{figure}[!ht]
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth, center]{../plots/14_14.pdf}
    \caption{Euler method solution to differential equation \label{fig14_14}} 
\end{figure}
