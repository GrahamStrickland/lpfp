Applying (14.16) repeatedly to the given figures, we have
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        v(1\,s) &= v(0\,s + \frac{F^{tv}_{net}\bigl(0\,s, v(0\,s)\bigr) (1\,s)}{1 \text{kg}} \\
              &= 2.0000 \, \text{m}/\text{s} + \bigl(F_1(0) + F_2(2.0000)\bigr) \text{m}/\text{s} \\
              &= 2.0000 \, \text{m}/\text{s} + \bigl(4\cos{0} - 3(2.0000)\bigr) \text{m}/\text{s} \\
              &= 0.0000 \, \text{m}/\text{s} \\
        v(2\,s) &= v(1\,s + \frac{F^{tv}_{net}\bigl(1\,s, v(1\,s)\bigr) (1\,s)}{1 \text{kg}} \\
              &= 0.0000 \, \text{m}/\text{s} + \bigl(F_1(1) + F_2(0.0000)\bigr) \text{m}/\text{s} \\
              &= 0.0000 \, \text{m}/\text{s} + \bigl(4\cos{1} - 3(0.0000)\bigr) \text{m}/\text{s} \\
              &= 2.1612 \, \text{m}/\text{s} \\
