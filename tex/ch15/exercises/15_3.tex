Using the Euler method with
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        \frac{dx(t)}{dt} &= v(t) \\
        \frac{dv(t)}{dt} &= -3x(t) + 4\cos{2t} - 2v(t),
    \end{split}
\end{equation*}
subject to the initial conditions $x(0) = 2$ and $v(0) = 1$, we have the following:
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        x(0.1) &= x(0) + v(0)\Delta t \\
               &= 2.0000 + (1.0000)(0.1) \\
               &= 2.1000 \\
        v(0.1) &= v(0) + \left(-3x(0) + 4\cos{2t(0)} - 2v(0)\right)\Delta t \\
               &= 1.0000 + \left((-3) \cdot 2.0000 + 4\cos{(2 \cdot 0)} - 2 \cdot 1.0000\right)(0.1) \\
               &= 0.6000 \\
        x(0.2) &= x(0.1) + v(0.1)\Delta t \\
               &= 2.1000 + (0.6000)(0.1) \\
               &= 2.1600 \\
        v(0.2) &= v(0.1) + \left(-3x(0.1) + 4\cos{2t(0.1)} - 2v(0.1)\right)\Delta t \\
               &= 0.6000 + \left((-3) \cdot 2.1000 + 4\cos{(2 \cdot 0.1)} - 2 \cdot 0.6000\right)(0.1) \\
               &= 0.2420 \\
        x(0.3) &= x(0.2) + v(0.2)\Delta t \\
               &= 2.1600 + (0.2420)(0.1) \\
               &= 2.1842 \\
        v(0.3) &= v(0.2) + \left(-3x(0.2) + 4\cos{2t(0.2)} - 2v(0.2)\right)\Delta t \\
               &= 0.2420 + \left((-3) \cdot 2.1600 + 4\cos{(2 \cdot 0.2)} - 2 \cdot 0.2420\right)(0.1) \\
               &= 0.9940.
    \end{split}
\end{equation*}
